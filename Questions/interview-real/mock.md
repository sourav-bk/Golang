> “In the next four years, I see myself as a strong technical professional and a senior backend engineer, taking ownership of complex projects and contributing to system design and architecture. 2. Tell me about yourself / What are your strengths?

> “I’m a backend developer with around 5 years of IT experience, including around 3 years of hands-on experience in Golang and Azure.
